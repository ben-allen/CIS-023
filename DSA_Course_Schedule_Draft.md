## Design notes
- **Java review**: 3 videos (1.5 weeks) as requested, ending right as the "real" course content begins with 1.1.
- **Pacing**: given the uneven incoming preparation, I split two of the historically trickier sections (1.3 Stacks/Queues, 3.3 Balanced Search Trees) across two videos instead of one.
- **Two midterms**: Midterm 1 (week 8) covers Fundamentals + Sorting (Ch. 1–2); Midterm 2 (week 15) covers Searching + Graphs (Ch. 3–4). Each gets its own review video the week before. Neither midterm week has a new-content video in that slot — just review, then the exam window itself.
- **Chapter 5**: kept String Sorts (5.1), Tries (5.2), Substring Search (5.3), and Regular Expressions (5.4); dropped Data Compression (5.5). Substring Search and Regex are combined into one video to fit the remaining slots — let me know if you'd rather cut Regex entirely and give Substring Search its own full video instead.
- **Week 14 (Thanksgiving)**: only one video (the Midterm 2 review), landing early in the week before the 11/25–29 break.
- There's no dedicated cumulative review video before finals — if you want one, the easiest place to free up a slot is cutting Regular Expressions from week 16.
- **Chapter 6 (Context)** isn't included, per your scope of chapters 1–5.
